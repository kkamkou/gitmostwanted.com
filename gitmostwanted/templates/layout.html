What's It All About?

Repositories often get hyped when they are created or promoted by big companies. Marketing, events, or other tricks can also push some projects into the spotlight. But this doesn’t always mean the repositories are actually useful.

For example, compare: Top vs GitHub search ordered by stars.

The gitmostwanted.com project analyzes GH Archive activity and highlights the most interesting repositories while filtering out noise. Read more in the Wiki.

Why login?

GMW acts as your personal radar where you curate liked repositories. Once there are 200+ users, it also suggests developers with similar preferences. You can sync your GitHub stars and share your public profile link (https://gitmostwanted.com/USER_NAME) with friends or teammates. Have fun!
